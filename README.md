# Retail-Store-Analytics-Project

📌 **Project Overview**
- This project focuses on analyzing and visualizing retail store data to uncover valuable business insights. It aims to help stakeholders understand customer behavior, sales trends, product performance, and inventory efficiency.

🎯 **Objectives**
- Analyze sales, customer, and product data

- Identify top-selling products and categories

- Understand customer purchase patterns

- Discover sales trends by region and time

- Provide data-driven recommendations for business growth


📊 **Dashboard Features**
This interactive dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of retail sales performance, customer preferences, and operational insights. Built using Tableau, the dashboard includes the following key components:

1. 📈 **Selling by Month (Line Chart)**
- Displays monthly profit trends throughout the year.

- Shows seasonality and peak-performing months (e.g., December shows highest profit).

- Helps identify low and high revenue periods for planning promotions.

2. 🏆 **Top 10 Products (Bar Chart)**
- Highlights the most profitable product sub-categories.

- Chairs and Phones are the top performers in terms of total profit.

- Useful for inventory and marketing focus.

3. 🧭 **Sales by Region (Map)**
- A U.S. map showing state-wise sales performance.

- Color intensity reflects profit levels.

- Helps identify regional performance gaps and opportunities.

4. 🌳 **Top Selling by Category (Tree Map)**
- Visualizes product categories by sales volume.

- Shows that Chairs, Phones, and Storage contribute significantly to total sales.

- Helps prioritize product lines based on revenue impact.

5. 🚚 **Ship Mode by Sales (Pie Chart)**
- Breaks down sales by shipping modes.

- Standard Class is the most frequently used, followed by Second Class.

- Insightful for logistics planning and customer delivery preferences.



6. 🔤 **Popular Keywords (Word Cloud)**
- Highlights frequently sold product sub-categories based on profit.

- Visual emphasis on items like Chairs, Phones, Binders, and Tables.

- A quick glance at popular product demand.
